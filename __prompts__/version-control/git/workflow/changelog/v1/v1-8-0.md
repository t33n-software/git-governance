### 3.1 Version `1.8.0`
[INTENT: SPECIFICATION]

**Classification**

|| Field | Value |
||-------|-------|
|| Semver Class | `minor` |
|| Breaking Change | `no` for the complete workflow bundle |
|| Rationale | The change is a backward-compatible ordering clarification across the bundle: the adapter keeps its full load mandate, entrypoint binding, and non-duplication laws, and the mutation embargo stays absolute; only the placement of read-only discovery and injected-prompt report obligations moves explicitly ahead of the worktree machinery. The same commit series additionally withdraws the redundant repository-local coverage prompt surface (`__prompts__/testing/coverage/prompt.md` with its two harness projections); that obligation is owned by the organization's system-rule white-box/coverage governance, so no workflow, authority, or safety contract of this bundle is removed — the removal is disclosed in the commit body of `chore(GOV-144): remove the redundant local coverage prompt`. |

**Migration / Consumer Impact**

No migration required. Consuming agents keep every gate; the adapter no longer displaces foreign-prompt report contracts behind its bootstrap machinery. Agents relying on the withdrawn local coverage prompt lose a duplicate surface only — the coverage obligation itself remains owned by the organization's system-rule governance and by the repository's configured quality gate.
